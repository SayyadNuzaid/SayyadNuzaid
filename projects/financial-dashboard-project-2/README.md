# Profitability & Product Performance Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard focused on profitability and unit-sales analysis.

## Business Objective

Analyze profitability, sales and units sold across products and countries.

## Analysis Areas

- Profit by country
- Profit by product
- Units sold
- Sales
- Manufacturing Price
- Sale Price

## Data Model

- financials

## Tools

- Power BI
- Financial analysis
- Business reporting
