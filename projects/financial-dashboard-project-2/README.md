# Profitability & Product Performance Dashboard

A Power BI project focused on **profitability, sales and unit-volume analysis** across products and countries.

## 🎯 Business Objective

Evaluate product and market performance by comparing profit, sales, units sold and pricing indicators.

## 📌 Key Analysis Areas

- Profit by country
- Profit by product
- Units sold
- Sales
- Manufacturing price
- Sale price
- Product and country comparison

## 🧩 Data Model

- **financials** — primary financial dataset

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Demonstrated

- Power BI
- Financial analysis
- Data modeling
- Product performance analysis
- Business reporting
- Interactive visualization

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard provides a management-friendly view of profitability and product performance, helping identify stronger products and markets and compare sales against volume and pricing.
